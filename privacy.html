Scorekeep privacy policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Scorekeep is a personal soundtrack tracker. Its playlist publisher, "Scorekeep publisher", is a private tool used only by the person who runs this site, to put playlists they built in Scorekeep into their own YouTube Music account.

What the publisher does with YouTube access

  • It creates private playlists in the owner's YouTube Music account and adds, removes, and renames tracks in the playlists it made.
  • It reads the owner's library only to find the playlists it made before, so a playlist is updated instead of duplicated.
  • It never deletes, edits, or reads anything else, and never posts, comments, likes, or subscribes.

What is stored, and where

  • The sign-in token that lets the publisher act for the owner is kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository and used only by its automated publish runs.
  • No YouTube data is collected, kept, sold, or shared with anyone. Nothing about the account leaves the owner's own YouTube Music, GitHub, and devices.
  • The Scorekeep app itself keeps its diary, ratings, and playlists in the browser's own storage on the owner's device. It uses no analytics, ads, or tracking.

Removing access

The access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party connections page. Once removed, the publisher can no longer do anything in the account.
